# Roadmap

## Purpose

Durable direction for `@codenhub/error`. The repo-wide roadmap is `docs/roadmap.md`.

## Not Planned

- **Canonical translation map** for the built-in presets' message keys. The public docs (Recipes, "Translate messages"; Caveats, "No translations ship with the package") settle that the keys are the contract and the words belong to the application's catalog. Shipping maps would add about 150 strings in pt-BR and es to keep in step with every English wording change, and no consumer has asked for them. Revisit when one does.
