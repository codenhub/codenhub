# 0.7.0

A button can be a liquid glass pane, the solo classes compose with presentation, intent, and elevation the way components do, the loaders are gone in favor of a spinner from an icon set, and a round of fixes to nested layout gaps, dialog placement, table rows, right-to-left switches, field groups, and forced colors.

## Added

- **Liquid glass buttons.** A `.btn` carrying `.glass-liquid` itself, in a glass scope, takes the blur, lens, and rim of a liquid pane, with a lens sized for a control. The fill is still the presentation's: `.solid` is an opaque plate under the rim, `.ghost` and `.soft` are clear panes. A liquid region alone still leaves its controls solid. See [Aesthetics → Liquid buttons](../usage/aesthetics.md#liquid-buttons).
- **Solo classes compose with presentation, intent, and elevation.** Fill and edge come from the element or a container, intent and elevation from the element's own class, so `.pixel-solo.edgeless` drops its ring, `.glass-solo.solid.success` is an opaque green pane, and `.neobrutalism-solo.flat` has no slab. With none of those classes a solo element paints what it did. See [Aesthetics → Solo classes](../usage/aesthetics.md#solo-classes).
- **`--font-mono` is a documented token.** It sets the font of the `kbd`, `code`, and `pre` helpers and of `<kbd>`, `<code>`, and `<pre>` under `/native`, and joins the other font tokens in [Theming → Fonts](../usage/theming.md#fonts), which also shows how to load a web font.
- **Linking without a bundler.** [Integrating → Without a bundler](../integrating/without-a-bundler.md) covers plain HTML, Rails, Django, and a CDN `<link>`, which load the compiled files by path rather than by package name.
- **A stated versioning policy.** Below 1.0 a minor release may break and a patch release never does; see [Versioning](../index.md#versioning).

## Changed

- **A solo element inside a `.solid`, `.soft`, or `.ghost` container takes that fill and the theme's text color**, since presentation cascades to it now as it does to components. A solo element whose own background does not follow the theme -- a dark toast on a light page -- inside such a container prints the theme's text; keep it out of the container, or give it `.solid` with an intent.
- **`.tight` and `.loose` stay on their own element.** They wrote `--layout-gap`, which inherits, so a plain `.stack`, `.view`, `.cluster`, or `.auto-grid` nested inside one took its gap. A nested layout keeps the default gap now; set `--layout-gap` on a container to change the gap of every layout inside it.
- **Your margin places a component dialog.** The centring of an open `<dialog class="card">` (or `.panel`, `.surface`) overruled your margin utilities and unlayered CSS; it now sits in `utilities` at zero specificity, so `mt-8` or your own rule wins, and it still beats a `base` reset such as Preflight's. A margin reset in a cascade layer of your own, declared after the package's, now wins over the centring too, as it already does over every component property.
- **`.cyber` glows from the outline.** Its glow is the intent color at 60% with a 1px spread, up from 40% with none, so the brightest point sits on the element's outline. An edgeless button, badge, or panel read as backlit -- a shadow on a dark page -- because the glow just outside it was brighter than its own fill. `.cyber-solo` takes the same glow. In the light theme the near-black glow of a neutral component is heavier too; a hued `--cyber-ink` is still the way to make it read as light there.
- **The neutral palette values carry no hue.** `--palette-border`, `--palette-surface`, and `--palette-text` are `oklch(… 0 none)` in both themes, where they were `oklch(… 0 0)`. The colors themselves are unchanged, but a missing hue takes its partner's in polar interpolation: `color-mix(in oklch, var(--palette-text), oklch(60% 0.2 240))` now lands on hue 240, a blue, where it travelled from hue 0 and landed on a purple. Mix in `oklab`, or give the neutral an explicit hue, to keep the old result.

## Removed

- **The loaders.** `.loader`, its `.sm` and `.lg` sizes, the nine variants (`.dots-wave`, `.dots-fade`, `.dots-queue`, `.dots-rotate`, `.dots-grow`, `.dots-grow-alternate`, `.dot-bounce`, `.bars-wave`, `.pulse-ring`), `.loader-mask`, and the `@codenhub/styles/tw/loader` entrypoint are gone. Importing `/tw/loader` now fails to resolve, and `<span class="loader dots-wave">` in the complete CSS renders nothing. `.sm` and `.lg` on other components are unchanged. Use a spinner from an icon set instead, such as `@codenhub/icons`, which this package does not import: a glyph with `ic-anim-spin` (`ic-lucide-loader-circle ic-anim-spin`) or the animated `svg-spinners` family, both in `currentColor`. Under `prefers-reduced-motion: reduce` every loader showed a still ring; a glyph with `ic-anim-spin` stops there too, but `svg-spinners` artwork keeps moving, so choose the glyph where that matters. See [Feedback](../usage/feedback.md) for loading the icons.

## Fixed

- **A focused `.solid.primary` control under `.pixel` shows its focus.** The inset focus ring is the primary color, so it vanished into the fill; a line of the page color now sits inside it. `.pixel-solo` takes the same line.
- **Controls and surfaces under `.pixel` keep an edge in forced colors.** Pixel draws its edge as an inset shadow, which forced colors remove, so buttons, fields, and cards had no boundary; forced colors now draw a solid border and drop the clip. `.pixel-solo` draws an inward outline there.
- **A `.disabled` text field holds still under the pointer.** The class spelling of disabled still darkened an `.ipt`'s line on hover, unlike `disabled`, `aria-disabled`, and `data-disabled`.
- **`.sketch-rounded` beside `.sketch-solo` stays on the solo element.** Its radius tokens inherited into the element's contents, so a `.btn` inside a rounded sketch toast drew a sketch outline on a page with no sketch in scope.
- **A checked native radio looks like a checked `.radio` in forced colors.** Under `/native` it drew a Highlight ring and dot on Canvas, where `.radio` draws a Highlight disc with a HighlightText dot, so `<input type="radio" class="radio">` changed look with the entrypoint. Both draw the disc now.
- **`/theme` declares every documented color and foundation token**, except `--focus-ring-offset`, which is computed on the focused element, and the `/tw`-only `--breakpoint-xs`. The compiled entry left out `--color-background`, `--color-foreground`, `--color-text-secondary`, and `--font-default`, because nothing in it read them, so `var(--color-background)` in your own CSS over `/theme` alone resolved to nothing.
- **A liquid region blurs what takes no liquid layers.** A bare `<table>` under `/native` or your own `@apply surface` inside a `.glass-liquid` region took its clearer ground with nothing blurred behind it; it takes liquid's lighter blur now.
- **Liquid's corners stay off a solo element's contents.** `.glass-solo.glass-liquid` no longer hands liquid's corner tokens to what it holds, so a `.btn` inside a liquid solo toast keeps the page's corners.
- **A checked `.switch` stays in its track right to left.** Under `dir="rtl"` the checked knob moved the left-to-right way, out past the track's right edge; it sits at the inline end, the left, now.
- **A table row shows its own intent.** A `<tr class="destructive">` in a `.data-table.success` kept the table's green text, rule, and hover, because the table resolved them before the row's intent applied. A row with an intent draws them in its own now, still in contrast ink on a `.solid` table; other rows keep the table's.
- **A button in an `.input-group` keeps its own edge.** The field's line reached every component inside the group, so a `.ghost` or `.edgeless` clear button drew a border the field's color. It rests as it does outside the group now, and the group keeps its line.
- **An `.alert` in another aesthetic nested under `.neobrutalism` casts no slab.** Neobrutalism raised the elevation of every alert below it, so one inside a `.chunky-tile`, `.glass`, `.pixel`, `.cyber`, or `.sketch` region took neobrutalism's shadow, unlike a card there. It takes the nested aesthetic's look now.
- **A select keeps its drop-down arrow in forced colors.** The chevron is drawn with gradients, which forced colors remove, so in Chromium and Firefox a `.select` or a native `<select>` under `/native` read as a text field. A single-row select shows the platform's arrow there now.
- **An `.input-group` holding a disabled control holds still under the pointer.** The group dimmed and showed the not-allowed cursor, but its line still darkened on hover; it no longer does.
- **A padding modifier pads only its own card or panel.** `.p-xs`, `.p-sm`, `.p-lg`, and `.flush` on a `.card` or `.panel` reached every card and panel nested inside it, so a card in a `.card.p-xs` sat at 8px instead of its 20px, and one in a `.flush` card had none. A nested surface keeps its own padding now; give it a modifier of its own to change it.
- **A long select label in an `.input-group` stops short of the chevron.** The group took the select's inline padding, so a label too long for the field ran under the drop-down arrow, at the right or, right to left, at the left. A single-row select keeps room for its chevron at the inline end now.
- **Typed text in a hued or invalid field reads at 4.5:1.** The text in `.ipt`, `.textarea`, and `.select` was the intent's base color, which fell as low as 2.5:1 against the field: a `.warning` field in the light theme, and `.success`, `.destructive`, `.info`, and `aria-invalid="true"` fields in the dark one, worst on `.soft` and `.solid`. It is the intent's strong tone now, darker in light and lighter in dark, at 4.5:1 or better for every intent and presentation in both themes. The field's line, focus ring, and fill are unchanged. Native fields under `/native` take the same text. A field with no intent class takes the neutral strong tone too, so its text is a shade different from 0.6.
- **The package's NOTICE covers the checkbox marks' Feather origin.** The checkbox check and indeterminate minus come from Lucide icons derived from Feather, and NOTICE now carries Feather's MIT notice beside Lucide's ISC one.
