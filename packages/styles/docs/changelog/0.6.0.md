# 0.6.0

A liquid-glass modifier, glass that keeps every presentation's fill whole, and a round of fixes to tables, controls, and the focus ring.

## Added

- **`.glass-liquid`**, a modifier of `.glass`: continuous corners, a bright rim, a lighter blur, a clearer ground, and a lens that bends what is behind a surface at its edges (Chromium today; Firefox and Safari keep the rim, blur, and ground). Put it beside `.glass` on the region, or on one surface inside a glass region; `.glass-solo.glass-liquid` paints the same pane on an element the package does not style. Without `.glass` in scope it changes the corners and nothing else. See [Aesthetics → Liquid glass](../usage/aesthetics.md#liquid-glass).
- **`.unadorned`** removes the browser's own field decorations -- the date picker button, number spin buttons, and search clear button and magnifier -- on a control or on its `.input-group`, for a field whose adornments you draw yourself. See [Forms → Icons](../usage/forms.md#icons).
- **`[data-state="closed"]` on `.tooltip`** hides the bubble while it is hovered or focused, for an Escape handler, and **`--tooltip-linger`** (300ms) sets how long a bubble stays after the pointer leaves its trigger. See [Tooltips](../usage/tooltips.md).
- **`--ui-rule-ink`**, the neutral line color of a table's frame and rules, read ahead of `--ui-ink`. Glass sets it so a table's lines read on its plate; every other aesthetic clears it.

## Changed

- **Glass no longer thins fills.** It stopped setting `--ui-bg-alpha: 0.8`, so a `.solid` button, a checked toggle, and a `.solid` card are opaque under glass like everywhere else. Translucency comes from the surface ground alone. Glass buttons also take the base `scale(0.97)` press (it was `none`).
- **A neutral `.solid` rests on the page background.** Its capped 20% fill was painted over nothing and showed whatever was behind it; it is now an opaque grey. The generated `--palette-neutral-solid-bg` and `-bg-hover` values changed to match, in both themes.
- **Tables frost under glass and keep their lines.** A default or `.soft` `.data-table` blurs like a surface, its frame and rules read `--ui-rule-ink`, and a bare `<table>` under `/native` reads it too.
- **A table takes the surface corner and is never clipped.** The four corner cells round instead of an `overflow` clip, so a `<caption>` is untouched in every engine; a cell spanning rows into a bottom corner leaves that corner square. `.table-wrap` makes room for an aesthetic's halo (cyber's glow) inside its scroll box, so put the aesthetic class on the wrapper or an ancestor.
- **Table rules draw at the aesthetic's line width**, uncapped, so `.chunky-tile`'s 2px frame no longer sits over a 1px row rule and `.pixel` draws them at all.
- **The focus ring is pulled in by the focused element's line width.** `--focus-ring-offset` is no longer declared at `:root`; its default is computed on the element, so it lands on the edge under every aesthetic. Setting `--focus-ring-offset` yourself still wins, but reading `var(--focus-ring-offset)` in your own CSS now needs a fallback.
- **Ink aesthetics draw controls in the control border.** Under `.neobrutalism`, `.pixel`, `.cyber`, and `.sketch`, a resting neutral field or toggle uses `--color-control-border` instead of the near-primary ink. A bare native checkbox or radio under `/native` takes the control ink too, and a `.neutral` field under glass takes it like an unclassed one.
- **Text controls keep the browser's own decorations.** The date picker button, number spin buttons, and search clear button were hidden on every text control, which also removed what they do. They are back by default; add `.unadorned` to remove them.
- **Buttons and fields take the surrounding font on every entrypoint.** `<button>` under `/native`, and every `.btn` and text control under `/components`, used the user agent's smaller control font (13.33px), so the same label printed at three sizes across elements and entrypoints. They inherit the page's font now, as they already did on `.`.
- **A default `.btn` is `--control-height` tall**, 40px, the same as `.ipt` beside it; it was 42px, and 44px under a 2px aesthetic line. Its vertical padding gives back the line width.
- **`/tw` keeps Tailwind's `2xl` breakpoint.** It redefined `--breakpoint-2xl` as 90rem, which moved every `2xl:` utility in a consumer's own Tailwind build from 96rem. `--breakpoint-xs` (30rem) is still added. Nothing in the package used the override, so the compiled entrypoints are unchanged.
- **Components honour reduced motion and fix `<dialog>` on every entrypoint.** `/components` and the focused `/tw/*` entries carry no reset, so the skeleton and progress shimmer, the indeterminate sweep, and the tooltip, toggle, and press transitions kept moving under `prefers-reduced-motion: reduce`, and a `<dialog class="card">` rendered open on load. Each component now stops its own motion, and the `<dialog>` fix ships beside `.card`. The fix now reaches only a dialog carrying `.card`, `.panel`, or `.surface`, so a dialog the package does not style keeps its own margin.
- **A tooltip bubble is hoverable.** It lingers for `--tooltip-linger` after the pointer leaves the trigger and stays open while the pointer is over it, as WCAG 1.4.13 asks; it used to vanish on the way. A closed bubble is `visibility: hidden` rather than only transparent, so a bubble no `aria-describedby` points at is no longer read in place while closed.
- **The Tailwind CSS peer range is `>=4.3.2 <5`.** It claimed `>=4.0.0`, but the `/tw` source needs `@source not` and `@source inline()` (Tailwind 4.1) and is tested against 4.3.2. The peer is still optional, and only `/tw` needs it.
- **Browser floor is Firefox 128.** Non-inheriting elevation relies on `@property`; the documented floor was Firefox 121.

## Fixed

- **A switch's knob sits inside `.pixel`'s inset ring** instead of covering it, so an unchecked and a checked switch no longer read as two halves.
- **The sketch solo shadow is cast in the depth colour** rather than full ink.
- **No stray `.contents` rule.** The compiled `.`, `/native`, and `/components` stylesheets shipped Tailwind's `.contents{display:contents}`, picked up from the license text; the build no longer scans the package's root files.
- **Links under `/native` keep the size of their text.** `a` was mapped to body copy, so a link inside a heading printed at 14px and regular weight.
- **The Tailwind guide no longer imports Tailwind twice.** `/tw` imports Tailwind itself, so it replaces your own `@import "tailwindcss"`; with both, an unminified build carried two copies of Preflight. The focused `/tw/*` entrypoints and `/tw/native` still expect your import first.
- **`.tooltip-icon` keeps its size on a `<button>`.** The docs now use a real `<button type="button">` as the trigger, since `aria-label` on a role-less `<span>` is not reliably announced; the chip resets the padding and minimum size a button brings under `/native` and `/components`. The docs also no longer call it circular under every aesthetic: it is square under `.neobrutalism` and `.pixel`.
- **A wrapped `.hint` has room between its lines.** Its line height equalled its 14px size, so wrapped lines touched; it takes `text-sm`'s 20px leading now.
- **`/palette` follows a light subtree nested in a dark one.** Its light values sat on `:root` alone, so a `.light` region inside `.dark` kept the dark values. They answer the explicit light selectors too now.
- **Tables align to the inline start.** `.data-table` cells and its caption used `text-left`, so a right-to-left table stayed left-aligned.
- **A region of one aesthetic nested inside another keeps its own material.** Every aesthetic now restates the depth colour and clears the focus inset, hover transform, and shadow tokens it does not use, so glass inside `.chunky-tile` no longer casts a black slab under every card, glass inside `.cyber` casts its shadow again, and a region inside `.pixel` no longer inherits pixel's inset focus layer.
